# Muzzle

![Muzzle banner](docs/assets/readme-banner.png)

Your Mac has speakers.

Your meeting has people.

Your playlist was not on the agenda.

Muzzle is a tiny macOS menu bar app that mutes your built-in speakers during risky moments, like when your headphones disconnect or your Mac wakes up.

## What It Does

- Mutes built-in speakers when headphones disconnect
- Mutes built-in speakers after your Mac wakes up
- Lets headphones, AirPods, Bluetooth audio, USB audio, and displays keep working normally
- Lets you pause protection for 5 or 30 minutes
- Lives quietly in the menu bar

## Why

Mute only works if you remember to use it.

This app exists for the moments when you are opening your laptop, joining a call, walking into a meeting, or generally trusting macOS a little more than you should.

It is also useful for protecting the privacy of your playlists, autoplaying videos, suspiciously loud tabs, and other questionable research.
